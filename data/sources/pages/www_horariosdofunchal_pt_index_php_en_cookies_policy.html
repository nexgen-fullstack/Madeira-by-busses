COOKIE POLICY

This cookie policy aims to inform you clearly and precisely about the cookies we use, detailing below what a cookie is, what it is used for, what types of cookies we use, their purpose, and how you can configure or disable them if you wish.

Reading this policy is important, as by continuing to browse our website you agree to the use of cookies in your browser and devices used, unless you have disabled them.

WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are files created by the websites you visit. They make your online experience easier by storing browsing information. With cookies, sites can keep your session logged in, remember your site preferences, and provide you with locally relevant content.

WHAT IS THEIR PURPOSE?

The use of cookies aims to improve the user's browsing experience, increasing the speed and efficiency of website responses. It eliminates the need to repeatedly enter the same information and helps determine usefulness, interest, and the number of uses. Each cookie has a function and an expiration date.

Refusing to use cookies on the website may result in the inability to access some of its areas or to receive personalized information.

TYPES OF COOKIES USED

There are session cookies (temporary duration) and persistent cookies (long duration).

The former are used only during the period you browse a particular site, after which they are deleted, and allow you to maintain your navigation on that site without having to log in again, even if you change pages. The latter remain stored on your device even after you finish browsing the site, and aim to allow you to return to that site without the settings you initially defined, such as language, being changed in this new navigation.

Cookies can also be categorized according to the various purposes associated with them, regardless of whether they are first-party cookies (cookies installed by the site you are visiting) or third-party cookies (cookies installed by other sites that are not the one you are browsing).

Thus, for example, we may have:

Cookie TypeDescription
Necessary Cookies These cookies are always used, regardless of your consent, as they are strictly necessary for the regular functioning of the site. Necessary cookies aim to ensure operability, optimization, and security issues and are, as a rule, persistent cookies.
Preference Cookies This type of cookie stores user preference settings, such as language, so you do not have to configure them every time you browse the site; they identify the user through a unique identification number (ID), associated with the login time, allowing you to browse the site or other Internet pages open in the browser without having to log in constantly; and they allow counting the number of visits to the site, through the collection of users' IP, thus enabling statistical studies on the quality of the service provided on that site. By their nature, these cookies may be temporary, although they tend to be persistent.
Analytical/Statistical Cookies These are cookies that allow quantifying the number of users of a site and analyzing their behavior, in order to define strategies for improving the service provided. As a rule, they are third-party cookies.
Advertising/Marketing Cookies This type of cookie aims to collect information about the use made of the site (e.g., clicks made and most viewed ads), allowing to identify user preferences so that the advertising directed to them meets their interests. They are mostly persistent cookies.

HOW TO DISABLE THE USE OF COOKIES?

You can revoke consent or modify preferences regarding cookie management at any time on the site's cookies page.

Alternatively, most browsers allow control over cookies stored on the visitor's devices, as well as their immediate deletion, if the user wishes to stop allowing local cookie storage. For this, you can find more information on the sites: www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

Users can always configure the devices and browsers they use to accept all or some cookies, to be notified whenever a cookie is issued, or to never receive cookies.

You can find more specific information on how to manage cookie preferences for the most popular browsers at:

  • Google Chrome
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Internet Explorer
  • Opera
  • Apple Safari

You can always find information regarding cookie management for other browsers by visiting the provider's site.
